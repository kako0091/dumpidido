BABEL

A structure made of what it is not.

Iteration 1

Three vocabularies, one painting

Sky: enclosed, domestic and architectural objects. Tower: organic life, weather, water and motion. Ground: intangible states, time and abstractions. Each region draws from its own shuffled vocabulary, and words are replaced continuously.

These are constrained categories of absence and contrast, not literal dictionary antonyms. They were selected for this particular Bruegel painting. Other uploaded pictures use the same spatial map as an experiment, but their regions may not match.

The procedure

The painting supplies the form, but its subjects are excluded from the words describing them. Region masks are drawn manually for this particular Tower of Babel. The local-detail adjustment preserves differences between neighboring light and dark architectural features instead of flattening them into black and white.

Words are shuffled independently within each region. Each regeneration changes the text, not the underlying image. Hold the original button to compare.
